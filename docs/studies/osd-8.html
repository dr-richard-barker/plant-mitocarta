Biological Synopsis & Spaceflight Discoveries

Simulated galactic cosmic ray exposure using heavy relativistic ions (56Fe, 28Si) and proton beams at the NASA Space Radiation Laboratory. Evaluates deep-space radiation hazards on mitochondrial Fe-S cluster stability, organellar DNA double-strand breaks, and the mobilization of dual-targeted homologous recombination repair machinery (RecA1, PolIA).

Key Biological Insights:

Key Discovery: Profound vulnerability of mitochondrial iron-sulfur [4Fe-4S] clusters in Complex I and Complex II to heavy ion oxidation.
Key Discovery: Strong induction of dual-targeted genome maintenance enzymes (RecA1 +1.15 log2FC, p = 0.0008).
Key Discovery: Mitochondria-to-nucleus retrograde stress wave triggering systemic antioxidant defenses.
Key Discovery: Enhanced stromule contact site dynamics between plastids and mitochondria under radiation damage.
